Attaching the Trainees to Public Institutions for Practical Training 
Your attention is hereby drawn to Public Administration Circular No. 04/2017 dated 28.02.2017, 
Public Administration Circular No. 04/2017 (I) dated 10.08.2017 and Public Administration 
Circular No. 04/2017 (II) dated 04.06.2025 issued on the above matter.  
02. Paragraph 08 of Public Administration Circular No. 04/2017 dated 28.02.2017 is deleted and 
Paragraphs 02 and 06 are amended in the following manner. Other provisions stipulated in the said 
circular as well as in Public Administration Circular No. 04/2017 (I) dated 10.08.2017 and Public 
Administration Circular No. 04/2017 (II) dated 04.06.2025 shall remain unchanged.  
02.  Authority is hereby delegated to all Secretaries of Ministries, Chief Secretaries of 
Provinces and District Secretaries to approve applications submitted for obtaining practical 
training by students, who have qualified after completing courses at government training 
institutes, such as the Vocational Training Authority of Sri Lanka, National Youth Services 
Council, National Apprentice and Industrial Training Authority, Technical Colleges and 
Computer Laboratories operating in schools under the Ministry of Education, Zonal 
Computer Resource Centres and Provincial Information and Communication Technology 
Centres as well as undergraduates in their second, third and fourth years following degree 
programmes at foreign or state universities recognized by the University Grants 
Commission, universities established under an Act of Parliament of Sri Lanka or degree 
courses recognized under Section 25A of the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978. 
06.  All practical trainings shall strictly be limited to a maximum period of 06 months. 
However, in case where the prescribed practical training period of a course exceeds 06 
months, such training shall not exceed the prescribed training period of the said course. 
03.  This circular is issued with the concurrence of the General Treasury.
