REVENUE ESTIMATES FOR YEAR 2027 FOR REVENUE CODE 20.02.01.01, RENT 
ON GOVERNMENT BUILDINGS & HOUSING 
 
Your attention is drawn to Circular No:01/2015 dated 20.07.2015 issued by the Department 
of Fiscal Policy and revisions made to the same, which consist of guidelines in respect of 
estimating, collecting, supervising and reporting government revenue. 
 
 
02.   The estimates on revenue expected to be collected by the Chief Accounting                    
Officers/ Accounting Officers, who have been entrusted with the responsibility to collect 
revenue under revenue code 20.02.01.01, revenue of rent on government buildings & 
housing, for which 
the Secretary of the Ministry‍ of‍ Public‍ Administration,                        
Provincial Councils and Local‍Government acts as the Revenue Accounting Officer, should 
be prepared as realistic and accurate as possible as per F.R. 85 (1), (2) and submitted to the 
“Chief 
Finance 
Officer” 
of 
the 
Ministry‍
of‍
Public‍
Administration,                         
Provincial Councils and Local‍ Government along with a soft copy to the e-mail address 
"buildingrentpubad@gmail.com".       
 
03. 
Therefore, you are kindly informed to prepare estimates on revenue expected to be 
collected by your office for the year 2027 under  revenue code 20.02.01.01, revenue of rent 
on government buildings & housing, as per the annexed format and submit the same to this 
Ministry on or before 30.06.2026.
