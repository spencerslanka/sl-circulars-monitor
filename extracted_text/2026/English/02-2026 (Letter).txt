Utilization of Information Technology Tools for Daily Duties 
 
The use of Information Technology tools is an optimal solution for most of the issues faced by 
the general public as service recipients in obtaining government services and by government 
officers as service providers in delivering the said services.  
 
02.  The Ministry of Digital Economy has published a set of instructions including a simple 
explanation on the Information Technology tools that can be utilized to maintain an effective 
public service with proper time management and the manner in which such tools should be used. 
 
03.   The set of instructions containing simple guidelines on Information Technology tools and 
their usage has been published under 'Downloads' of the official website of the Ministry of 
Digital Economy at https://mode.gov.lk and the relevant guidelines can be downloaded through 
the following links. 
 
Sinhala - https://mode.gov.lk/si/guidelines/Docs/modeigsin.pdf 
 
Tamil - https://mode.gov.lk/ta/guidelines/Docs/modeigtam.pdf 
 
English - https://mode.gov.lk/guidelines/Docs/modeigeng.pdf 
 
04.   You are informed to apprise all Heads of Institutions under your purview on the above set 
of instructions on Information Technology tools and their usage and to take action to encourage 
the utilization of these Information Technology tools for their daily duties as applicable.
