List of Disciplinary Inquiry Officers Consisting of Attorneys-at-Law under 
Sub-Section 19:5 of Chapter XLVIII, Volume II of the Establishments Code 
 
The list of attorneys-at-law who may act as disciplinary inquiry officers in formal disciplinary 
inquiries conducted in respect of public officers, in accordance with the provisions of sub-
section 19:5 of Chapter XLVIII of Volume II of the Establishments Code, is set out in 
Schedule 01.  
 
02. Public Administration Circular No. 35/92(II) dated 26.06.1995, Public Administration 
Circular No. 35/92(IV) dated 13.11.1997, and Public Administration Circular No. 35/92(VI) 
dated 02.12.2022, under which panels of attorneys-at-law were published to date, are hereby 
cancelled with effect from the date of issue of this Circular, without causing prejudice to the 
formal disciplinary inquiries currently being conducted by tribunals appointed under the said 
Circulars. 
 
03. Attorneys-at-law included in this panel cannot act as defending officers or prosecuting 
officers in instances specified in Sub-section 19:14 of Chapter XLVIII, Volume II of the 
Establishments Code. 
 
04. Attorneys-at-law who have over ten years of experience as an attorney-at-law and are 
not more than 65 years of age are eligible to apply for this panel. Applications must be 
submitted using the application form set out in Annex 01 of this Circular, along with a Letter 
of Good Standing issued by the Supreme Court. 
 
05.   The applications and documents submitted accordingly are scrutinized by a committee 
functioning under this Ministry, and this Circular is updated annually by including in the panel 
of Disciplinary Inquiry Officers only those attorneys-at-law who satisfy the relevant 
qualifications. 
06. Attorneys-at-law whose names appear in this panel must, in conducting disciplinary 
inquiries, pay due attention to the provisions in Sub-sections 19:11, 19:12, and 19:13 of

Chapter XLVIII, Volume II of the Establishments Code, and any violation of the said 
provisions shall constitute grounds for removal from the panel. 
 
 
07. Furthermore, the said attorneys-at-law must submit a Letter of Good Standing issued by 
the Supreme Court once every 05 years, along with the covering letter provided in Annex 02 
of this Circular. Failure to submit such letters shall result in the removal of the said attorneys-
at-law from the panel, and the list of attorneys-at-law so removed is indicated in Schedule 02. 
 
08. Furthermore, Schedule 01 does not impose any restriction on the districts where formal 
disciplinary inquiries may be conducted. If any information specified therein (only the 
address, telephone number, language medium for conducting inquiries, and districts where 
inquiry officers have expressed preference to conduct disciplinary inquiries) needs to be 
amended, a request to that effect must be submitted in writing to the Director General of 
Establishments. 
 
09. Payments for formal disciplinary inquiries shall be determined based on the Public 
Administration Circular No. 27/2025 dated 24.10.2025, and the provisions mentioned below 
must be made applicable only to Paragraphs 03 and 07 of that Circular, subject to the 
provisions stipulated in Chapter XIV of the Establishments Code. 
 
        9.1. Officers traveling on duty to conduct formal disciplinary inquiries may obtain 
mileage allowance for the whole or a part of the journey, subject to the rates set out 
in Sub-section 8:2 of Chapter XIV, Volume I of the Establishments Code, as 
revised by Public Administration Circular No. 10/2026 dated 09.04.2026. 
 
  9.2.  Furthermore, subject to the provisions of Chapter XIV of Volume I of the 
Establishments Code, as amended by Public Administration Circular 20/2018 
dated 21.08.2018, combined allowances can be obtained based on the maximum 
rate.
