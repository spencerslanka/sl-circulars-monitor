Granting of No-Pay Leave to Officers Entitled to the Exercise of Political 
Rights Who Contest the Local Government Elections and Are Elected as 
Deputy Mayors / Vice Chairmen 
 
Your attention is drawn to Public Administration Circular 12/2021 dated 25.06.2021, issued 
amending Subsection 2:3:4 of Chapter XXXII of the Establishments Code. 
 
02. 
A decision has been taken at the meeting of the Cabinet of Ministers held on 
22.06.2026 to amend the provisions related to granting leave for officers entitled to the 
exercise of political rights who are elected as Deputy Mayors or Vice Chairmen after 
contesting local government elections. 
 
03. 
Accordingly, Subsection 2:3:4 of Chapter XXXII of the Establishments Code is 
amended by including the following new Subsection 2:3:4:1.
