Maintenance of Government Vehicles 
 
Your attention is drawn to sub section 2.1 of Public Administration Circular 05/2026 dated 
25.02.2026, issued under the subject “Maintenance of Government Vehicles”. 
 
02. 
In view of the fuel crisis that has arisen due to the prevailing conflict situation in the 
Middle East region, various state institutions have reported to this Ministry that owing to the 
restricted issuance of fuel following the introduction of the QR code system it has become difficult 
in certain instances to conduct the fuel consumption test by fully filling the vehicle's fuel tank, as 
stipulated in sub-clause 2.1 of the above-mentioned Public Administration Circular 05/2026 dated 
25.02.2026. 
 
03. 
Accordingly, based on the recommendations received from the Ministry of Energy in this 
regard, when taking action to obtain the fuel required for the government vehicle fuel consumption 
test under sub-clause 2.1 of Public Administration Circular 05/2026 dated 25.02.2026, actions 
should be taken to obtain fuel with the prior approval of the Ministry of Energy, in compliance 
with the fuel distribution and issuance system currently in force in the country. 
 
04. 
Accordingly, actions should be taken to obtain the fuel required for the fuel consumption 
test of government vehicles by submitting a formal request, along with the following information, 
to the Ministry of Energy. 
 
I. 
Quantity of fuel required 
II. 
Type of fuel required 
III. 
Registration numbers of the relevant vehicles 
IV. 
Time period for which fuel is required 
 
05. 
Furthermore, necessary measures must be taken to conduct the fuel consumption test in a 
manner that ensures the most economical use of the fuel obtained.
