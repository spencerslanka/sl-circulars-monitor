Revision of Fees for Translations, Interpretations and Typing 
 
Your attention is drawn to the fees mentioned in Public Administration Circular 12/2003(III) 
dated 22.08.2016 and 12/2003(IV) dated 27.02.2017 issued on the above matter. 
 
02. 
It has been decided at the meeting of the Cabinet of Ministers held on 30.06.2026 to 
revise the fees mentioned in the said circulars in the following manner. 
 
 
03. 
Translation fees 
          
 
(a) Translation fees 
I. 
A fee of Rs. 5.00 shall be paid per word translated irrespective of the 
category of the document.  
 
II. 
The relevant fee shall be paid depending on the number of words either in 
the document used for translation or the translated document. 
 
 
 
III. 
Your attention is drawn to Public Administration Circular 20/96 dated 
19.07.1996 and accordingly, in case of the institutions where there are no 
translators, arrangements shall be made on exigency of service to get the 
translation work performed by way of maintaining a list of resource 
persons with professional qualifications on translation.

04. 
Typing fees 
 
(a)  For typing a page containing,  
I. 
360 words or more 
 
Rs.90/- 
II. 
240 to 359 words 
 
Rs.80/- 
III. 
less than 240 words 
 
Rs.60/- 
 
(b) Stencil cutting per page  
 
Rs.100/- 
 
 
The above typing fees shall be paid for typing on the computer as well and relevant shall strictly 
be paid when work is performed on piece basis. 
 
05. 
The revised fees mentioned above shall be effective from 01.08.2026.
