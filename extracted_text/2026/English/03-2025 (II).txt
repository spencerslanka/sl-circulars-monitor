Registration of public officers under the Widows' and Orphans' / Widowers' and 
Orphans' Pension Scheme and the verification of their beneficiaries' information through 
a new online information system. 
Your attention is drawn to Public Administration Circular 03/2025 issued on 20.01.2025 and 
Public Administration Circular 03/2025 (I) issued on 17.11.2025 regarding the above matter. 
02. 
Accordingly, since the time period granted for the registration, updating of information, 
and verification of beneficiary details through the newly introduced online information system 
expired on 28.02.2026, the facility provided for the said activities in the aforementioned 
information system has been disabled since that date. 
03. 
However, considering the requests received from public institutions that were unable to 
complete these activities before 28.02.2026, and the necessity to include all public officers in 
the data system as this system for enrolling members in the Widows' and Orphans' Pension 
Scheme functions as the base database for services to be provided in the future through the 
Integrated Pensions Management System (iPMS) currently being implemented by the 
Department of Pensions, the aforementioned online information system has been reactivated till 
30.10.2026.
--- Page 2 ---
04. 
Therefore, all activities such as registration in the data system, updating information, 
and verification of beneficiary details must be completed by 30.10.2026. 
05. 
Since it has been observed that there are instances where incorrect data has been entered 
into the data system, special attention should be paid to creating a suitable monitoring 
mechanism to ensure that the staff is well-informed about the relevant circulars and to follow 
up on the accuracy of the data being entered into the system. 
06. 
Except for the amendments mentioned under paragraphs 03 and 04 above, all other 
provisions of Public Administration Circular 03/2025 and Public Administration Circular 
03/2025 (I) remain unchanged.
