Formalizing Solid Waste Management Activities in Government Institutions 
 
Your attention is drawn to Public Administration Circular 09/2025 dated 02.04.2025 issued on the 
above matter. 
 
02. 
Sub-section 2.1.2 of the said circular is hereby amended as follows: 
 
2.1.2 (a) Accordingly, the purchase and use of single-use plastic water bottles in all 
government institutions must be completely discontinued with effect from 31.05. 
2026. 
 
(b) All heads of institutions must take necessary measures to prevent the use of single-
use  plastics within their institutions that have been prohibited by the following 
Gazettes: 
 
i. Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka              
No. 2341/30 dated 19.07.2023 
 
ii. Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka             
No. 2034/37 dated 01.09.2017 
 
03. 
 Furthermore, the other provisions mentioned in the Public Administration Circular 
09/2025 issued on 02.04.2025 shall continue to be in force. 
 
 
04.  
This circular is issued with the concurrence of the Ministry of Environment.
